Demo 06

Sticky floating CTA

Slides in from the bottom-right after the user scrolls past 400px. A persistent campfire 🔥 icon and clear action — never blocks content, always available. Scroll down to see it appear.

Scroll this page to see the floating CTA pop in at the bottom-right corner. It stays visible until you scroll back near the top.

Why it works: the user reads, then forms intent. When intent forms, the action is already in reach — they don't have to scroll back up looking for a button.

Use it sparingly. One persistent CTA per page, max.
