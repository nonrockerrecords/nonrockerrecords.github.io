01

The fire was nearly out.

When Pinecrest first called us, their enrollment had been declining for three consecutive years. Their last agency had delivered a beautiful rebrand the year before — but the applications kept dropping. Something deeper was broken.

Their CMO put it plainly: "We know our story. We just can't figure out who's supposed to hear it."

02

The real problem wasn't the brand. It was the audience.

Three weeks into discovery, we'd interviewed 47 students, parents, and faculty. The pattern was clear: Pinecrest had been talking to the wrong applicants for years. Their identity was right — their targeting was wrong.

An 18% decline isn't a creative problem. It's a strategic one.

03

We didn't redesign anything. We rerouted everything.

Instead of another brand refresh, we rebuilt the audience model from scratch. New personas, new geographic priority markets, new channel mix. The brand stayed largely intact — but who saw it, and where, completely changed.

Then we activated, measured, and tuned in tight quarterly cycles.

04

Year one: applications up 42%.

Six months into the engagement, lead volume had recovered to pre-decline levels. Twelve months in, applications were up 42% year over year — the institution's strongest year in the past decade.

More importantly: the new applicants matched the personas we'd built. Yield rates climbed alongside volume.

05

Year two: efficiency tripled.

With the audience model proven, we doubled down on the channels that were working. Cost per lead dropped to a third of where it had been when we started. The marketing budget didn't grow — its impact did.

06

Where the story sits today.

Pinecrest is now in year three of the engagement. We've expanded into graduate marketing and retention work. Their President has spoken about our partnership at three industry conferences.

The fire's roaring.
