## Props bank

For photoshoots, music videos, live set dressing, merch backdrops, calendar shots. The seed direction (21 May 2026): crystal ball, skull, candles, darkness, oddities. This is nonrocker's **haunted / occult-Y2K** side — vaporwave still applies, but the palette opens up to include the dark sister of Y2K (mall-goth, Geocities witch, Hot Topic 1998, sailor-moon-coven, Lisa Frank meets the occult). Bright neon does not need to be the only register. The classical-bust-dripping-green-fluid motif in the existing artwork already lives in this territory; this just leans further in.

Note: several of these props (skull, candles, sustained darkness, drones-as-staging) overlap with the industrial/goth band stub (TASK00000700). That's fine — share the prop bank across bands, just shoot differently. nonrocker gets neon + scuzz + Y2K residue laid over the dark stuff. The goth band gets it clean and ceremonial.

### Core oddities (seed list)

- **Crystal ball** — literal glass one if findable cheap, OR a chrome gazing ball (garden-supply, $10-20), OR a CRT monitor with a swirling MS Paint vortex looping as the "ball." The fake-tech version is most on-brand.
- **Skull** — plastic anatomical (Halloween store, year-round availability decent), sugar skull (Dia de los Muertos crossover), MS Paint pixel skull printed and mounted, or actual decorated faux-skull props for individual shots.
- **Candles** — black tapers, dripping pillar candles, cathedral votives. Cheap pack from a craft store. Pre-burn them so the wax has character before the shoot.
- **Darkness** — single-source lighting, void backdrop (black sheet or velvet). Pairs naturally with monitor glow as the only other light. Treat darkness as a prop, not the absence of one.
- **Oddities-style smalls** — antique medical instruments (eBay), apothecary jars with murky liquid (food coloring + glycerin), preserved-specimen-looking jars (rubber bugs / plastic teeth in tinted water), taxidermy fakes (felt mice, plastic ravens), vintage anatomical posters (printable from Wikimedia public domain).

### Y2K-occult crossover (where this branch's identity lives)

- **Lava lamp as crystal ball** — the swirling wax is the divination surface. Bonus if the lava lamp is purple or green.
- **Magic 8 Ball** — propped open / cracked, with a custom answer card slot visible (`OUTLOOK: COMPILE ERROR`, `REPLY HAZY: TRY TURBOLAG`).
- **Ouija board with WASD keys** — homebuilt, plywood board with mechanical keycaps replacing the letters. The planchette is a 90s computer mouse.
- **Furby shrine** — multiple Furbies arranged on a candle-lit altar, eyes lit, framed like a saints' niche. Cursed-object energy is canonical for the era.
- **Tamagotchi memorial** — a small graveyard of dead Tamagotchis, each with a tiny tombstone listing date of digital death. Lit by candle.
- **CRT TV as scrying mirror** — old portable TV showing untuned static, framed in candles. The drummer "appears" in the static.
- **Custom tarot deck** — propped on a velvet cloth. Cards include `The Drummer (reversed)` / `The Soju` / `The Compiler` / `The Y2K Bug` / `The Statue` / `The Modem`. Designed as a future merch item; print a single deck first as a photoshoot prop.
- **Hourglass with weird sand** — replace the sand with glitter, beads, pixel-printed paper confetti, or color-graded soju (sealed). The hourglass is leaking time wrong.

### Mall-goth / Geocities-witch atmosphere props

- **Studded belts and chokers** as set dressing (not just costume) draped on candle holders.
- **Velvet drapery** as backdrop. Black, deep purple, or oxblood.
- **Sparkle / under-construction animated GIFs** projected onto the backdrop. Specifically the rotating cursor, the construction-worker animation, the dancing baby. Animated GIFs as lighting elements.
- **Pentagrams in MS Paint** — printed at poster size with visible JPEG compression, taped to the wall like a teenager's bedroom decor.
- **Spirit boards drawn in MS Paint** — same energy. Crooked, deliberately bad-mouse-drawn occult symbology.
- **Sailor Moon / Card Captor Sakura merch** as Y2K-coven set dressing. Crystal wand props, transformation lockets.

### Optical / light props

- **Blacklight UV** — single source. Highlights white fabric, certain paints, makes teeth glow. Cheap fluorescent fixtures or LED bars.
- **Cathedral light effect** — string Christmas lights behind a perforated black backdrop to mimic stained-glass scatter.
- **Strobe** (used carefully) for one or two shots only. Photograph the blur of motion against a still candle.
- **Monitor glow** — multiple monitors on, each showing a different Win95-era screensaver (Mystify, 3D Pipes, Flying Toasters, Starfield). Faces lit by the screensavers, color-shifted to whichever screen is closest.

### Costume hooks (set-dressing-adjacent)

- Velvet capes, sheer black robes, fishnet, jewelry layered over band tees, Hot Topic crucifix pendants, chunky platform boots.
- The drummer's costume: an empty chair with the costume *arranged* as if a person was sitting there.

## Photoshoot concept seeds

When TASK00000652 / photoshoot prep gets picked up (the prep doc currently lives Turbolag-side; nonrocker may want a parallel prep entry):

1. **Seance** — full group seated around a low table, candles, crystal ball / CRT, attempting to summon the drummer. The drummer's chair is empty but has an arranged costume.
2. **Each-member-as-tarot-card** — single-shot portraits using the custom deck design. The card frame is composited or built physically.
3. **Crystal-ball-CRT** — close-up of the chrome ball or CRT swirling, reflecting a band member's face distorted.
4. **Black-velvet-and-skull static portrait** — classical-painting composition, single light, baroque pose, but the member is holding a Game Boy instead of a memento mori.
5. **The altar shot** — full group not pictured; instead a fully-built altar (candles, skull, crystal ball, Furbies, Tamagotchis, soju bottles, kazoos, Win95 install disc) photographed as a still life. This is the album-cover candidate or the gatefold inner.
