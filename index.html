// 02_BIO.TXT

Who, allegedly

nonrocker (nonrocker Turbolag, Turbolag TurboLag, the matter is unresolved) formed in a Salt Lake basement somewhere between the second and third soju. The brief was simple: do not make rock. Make whatever the opposite of rock is, but with more kazoos and slightly more remorse.

The band operates at the intersection of vaporwave, digital hardcore, and the Sunday-morning feeling of waking up next to a Windows 95 install disc. Their debut LP Soju, Kazoos and Didgeridoos (NRK-2024) is engineered to crash whichever app you play it in. Reviews have been swift, brief, and largely sent from legal departments.

The rebrand to Turbolag has been in progress for an indeterminate number of months. The bassist insists it is two words. The drummer insists there is no drummer. We are not taking questions at this time.

Recorded somewhere between competence and chaos.
