01 // WHO, ALLEGEDLY

Formed in a Salt Lake basement

Somewhere between the second and third soju. The brief was simple: do not make rock. Make whatever the opposite of rock is, but with more kazoos.

The band operates at the intersection of vaporwave, digital hardcore, and the Sunday-morning feeling of waking up next to a Windows 95 install disc. Their debut LP Soju, Kazoos and Didgeridoos (NRK-2024) is engineered to crash whichever app you play it in.

Reviews have been swift, brief, and largely sent from legal departments.
