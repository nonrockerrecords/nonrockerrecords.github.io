// 02_BIO.TXT

Who, allegedly

Formed in a Salt Lake basement between the second and third soju. The brief was simple: do not make rock. Make whatever the opposite is, but with more kazoos and slightly more remorse.

They operate at the intersection of vaporwave, digital hardcore, and the Sunday-morning feeling of waking up next to a Windows 95 install disc. Soju, Kazoos and Didgeridoos is engineered to crash whichever app you play it in.
